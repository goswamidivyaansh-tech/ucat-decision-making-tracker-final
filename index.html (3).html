Work through each phase carefully — it takes ~50 minutes

Setup
Mock details
Enter the basics before you start the review process.
Phase 1 · 5 min
Immediate reflection
Before looking at results in detail — capture your raw feelings while they're fresh. Do this before you analyse anything.
Phase 2 · 10 min
Category results
Tally each category from the results screen. Green = correct, yellow = partial (1/2 marks), red = wrong. Add a note on time patterns — where did you spend too long, or panic click?
Phase 3 · 20–30 min
Question analysis
Go through each question and classify it. Write notes on the ones that matter — focus on Type A first (confident but wrong). Skip Type D (correct and confident) entirely.
Type A Confident but wrong — your logic is broken somewhere
Most important to review. Find the exact step where your logic diverged.
Type B Knew you were guessing or ran out of time
Ask: what earlier question stole the time that should have gone here?
Type C Got it right but felt unsure — was your reasoning actually correct?
Phase 4 · 10 min
Category pattern analysis
Write one sentence per category identifying your specific pattern today. This is what drives your next practice session.
Phase 5 · 5 min
Action notes
The three things that make this review actually change your next performance. Be specific. Look at these before every practice session until your next mock.
